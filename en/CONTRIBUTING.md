# Contributing to the C.H. Method

Critical contributions are welcome. The aim is not to shield the method from questions but to make it testable and better documented.

## Valuable contributions

Especially useful:

- reports on applying C.H. to different projects;
- cases where a W Slice worked or failed;
- evidence on supervision costs;
- conflicts between autonomy and review capacity;
- proposals to make criteria verifiable;
- comparisons with other methods;
- identification of unnecessary bureaucracy;
- evidence-backed historical corrections.

## Preservation rule

Do not silently edit files marked as historical versions. Corrections must preserve the original record or clearly explain the change.

## Proposals

For a conceptual change, describe:

- observed problem;
- context and available evidence;
- proposed change and expected impact;
- risk or limitation;
- how the proposal could be tested.

C.H. must remain open to criticism, reproducible, and independent of a specific tool.
