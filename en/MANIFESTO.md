# C.H. Manifesto 0.1

> Original historical version — August 27, 2026. English translation of [the Portuguese manifesto](../MANIFESTO.md).

We are learning better ways to develop software with people and intelligent agents by building real products and examining the results.

Through this experience, we have come to value:

- **verifiable intent** over lengthy prompts;
- **reproducible evidence** over confident responses;
- **small, reversible slices** over large autonomous deliveries;
- **explicit memory** over assumed context;
- **human accountability** over ownerless autonomy;
- **fit-for-purpose orchestration** over allegiance to a tool.

## Our commitment

AI can expand our ability to build. The responsibility to decide what is worth creating, prove that it works, and answer for its consequences remains human.
