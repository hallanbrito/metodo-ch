# Governance of the C.H. Method

C.H. is an experimental proposal open to criticism and evolution; its history must remain verifiable.

## Principles

- Historical versions must not be rewritten retroactively.
- Substantial conceptual changes must be explicitly versioned.
- Tool examples do not make any vendor a requirement.
- Contrary evidence must be documented, not concealed.
- Editorial and publication responsibility remains human.

## Accountability

**Hallan de Sousa Brito** holds human responsibility for publication and editorial decisions in this repository.

ChatGPT and other AI systems may assist with research, structuring, writing, analysis, review, and execution but do not assume moral, legal, or scientific responsibility.

## Evolution

Future proposals must distinguish:

1. **history** — what happened;
2. **principle** — conceptual rule;
3. **operational practice** — current, context-dependent implementation;
4. **experiment** — hypothesis still being validated.

This separation allows C.H. to evolve without confusing its origins with later versions.
