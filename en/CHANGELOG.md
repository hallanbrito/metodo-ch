# Changelog

All significant changes to the public C.H. Method documentation are recorded here. This is the English translation of [the Portuguese changelog](../CHANGELOG.md).

## [0.1] — 2026-08-27

### Historical milestone

- consolidation of Experimental White Paper v0.1;
- formalization of the name C.H. as **ChatGPT–Hallan** and **Hybrid Collaboration**;
- definition of manifesto values and twelve principles;
- creation of **W Slices** and the **CHAVE** cycle;
- autonomy levels CH-0 through CH-3;
- Evidence Packages and acceptance gates;
- Maestro Router as the initial case study.
