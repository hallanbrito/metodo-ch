# C.H. Method — Hybrid Collaboration

**Software with people + AI, without unnecessary complexity.** ☕  
[🇧🇷 Português](../README.md) · [🇬🇧 English](README.md)

> **One idea → one small delivery → test → review → learn.**

## The 5-step recipe

The **CHAVE** cycle fits in a cup of coffee:

1. **C — Contextualize:** inspect the code and the project's actual state.
2. **H — Harmonize:** agree on the goal, boundaries, and acceptance criteria.
3. **A — Act:** a human or AI implements one small **W Slice**.
4. **V — Verify:** test the change and review the evidence.
5. **E — Evolve:** accept the result and record what was learned.

**Who decides?** An accountable human. **Who executes?** Whoever is best suited, including AI. **Who proves it works?** Tests, reviews, and evidence — not just “done!”.

**Try it now:** choose **one small task** in an Issue. State the desired outcome and what must not change, implement it on a branch, test it, and open a PR. Merge only after review.

## Why it exists

C.H. **does not replace Scrum, Kanban, XP, or DevOps**. It draws on incremental delivery, simplicity, flow, tests, and feedback from established practices — and adds explicit boundaries for AI agents, versioned project memory, and evidence-based acceptance.

It emerged in 2026 from real development of [Maestro Router](examples/maestro-router/README.md). It is an **experimental proposal** open to criticism and improvement, not a scientifically proven superior method.
