# Origins of the C.H. Method

The C.H. Agile Method emerged in 2026 during incremental development of **Maestro Router**. Code generation stopped being the main bottleneck: defining decisions, limiting delegated scope, verifying results, and maintaining product coherence became the central work.

## The name

**C.H.** has two meanings:

1. **ChatGPT–Hallan** — records the original collaboration: Hallan de Sousa Brito as Product Owner, practitioner, and accountable human; ChatGPT as an architecture, analysis, and writing collaborator; and coding agents as bounded executors.
2. **Hybrid Collaboration** (Colaboração Híbrida) — generalizes the idea for other teams: people define purpose and remain accountable; agents expand analysis and execution capacity.

The dual meaning preserves the origin without tying the method to a vendor, model, or tool.

## Historical record

- **Practical origin:** development of Maestro Router.
- **Experimental white paper v0.1:** August 27, 2026.
- **Human conception and responsibility:** Hallan de Sousa Brito.
- **Conceptual collaboration and assisted writing:** ChatGPT (OpenAI).

C.H. does not claim to replace Scrum, Kanban, XP, or the Agile Manifesto. Its contribution is to make explicit the boundary between human intent and agentic execution.

## Central thesis

> In AI-assisted engineering, sustainable productivity is not the amount of code generated. It is the amount of value accepted with evidence, understanding, and accountability.

## Tool neutrality

The method is deliberately tool-neutral. A team may use different models, agents, and tools as long as execution follows verifiable boundaries and final accountability remains human.
