# Public Milestone — C.H. Method v0.1

**Public milestone date:** September 20, 2026 (Brazil)  
**Historical reference version:** 0.1  
**Original white paper:** August 27, 2026

This record closes the initial public foundation of the C.H. Method by applying its own **CHAVE** cycle.

## C — Contextualize

The C.H. Method emerged during development of Maestro Router and was consolidated in Experimental White Paper v0.1. Documentation existed, but there was not yet a dedicated public repository preserving its origin, manifesto, principles, artifacts, and evolution in a navigable, versioned way.

## H — Harmonize

The following decisions were established for the public foundation:

- preserve v0.1 as a historical record;
- distinguish history, principles, current practices, and experiments;
- remain neutral toward models and vendors;
- credit human conception and responsibility to Hallan de Sousa Brito;
- record conceptual collaboration and assisted writing by ChatGPT (OpenAI);
- allow study, sharing, and adaptation of documentation under CC BY 4.0;
- record later evolutions as new layers rather than rewriting the past.

## A — Act

The public repository `hallanbrito/metodo-ch` was created and organized.

Its foundation includes:

- README and navigation index;
- C.H. Manifesto 0.1;
- Experimental White Paper v0.1 in Markdown;
- origins and foundations;
- CHAVE cycle;
- W Slices;
- roles and autonomy levels;
- Evidence Packages and gates;
- governance and contribution guidelines;
- Maestro Router case study;
- reusable templates;
- changelog;
- CC BY 4.0 license;
- citation metadata;
- method evolution timeline.

## V — Verify

The public foundation was reviewed for:

- the presence of White Paper v0.1;
- preservation of the historical Manifesto;
- separation between v0.1 and later evolutions;
- documentation of core concepts;
- explicit license and citation;
- operational templates;
- traceability through Git commits.

**Economic C.H.** remains appropriately classified as an experimental hypothesis rather than a retroactive part of v0.1.

## E — Evolve

Version 0.1 becomes the public historical baseline.

From this milestone:

- historical corrections must be justified and traceable;
- new practices should be tested before becoming principles;
- negative results should also be documented;
- new versions should explain what changed and why;
- outside applications should be encouraged to test hypotheses.

> **The aim is not to preserve C.H. exactly as it was born. It is to preserve the truth of its origins and let the method evolve traceably.**

## Milestone status

**PUBLIC FOUNDATION v0.1 — COMPLETE**

Next horizon: experiment, measure, and mature the method toward a possible v0.2, with no predetermined date or content.
